Version 0.7.0
Engine polish. Contains:
- Physics: hinge angle limits (with restitution and softness, working with the motor) and ball-joint swing cone and
  twist limits for ragdolls, solved with the contacts and allocation-free; cylinder and convex hull colliders
  (quickhull with a vertex cap, solid mass and inertia, GJK/EPA with clipped face manifolds, raycasts, overlap
  queries, continuous-collision sweeps, mesh contacts, compound bodies)
- Rendering: sky (procedural gradient with a sun disc, or an equirectangular Radiance .hdr image) with image-based
  lighting baked once on the GPU (irradiance, GGX-prefiltered reflections, BRDF table); RenderSettings.sky,
  skyIntensity, skyRotation; HdrLoader
- UI: copy, cut and paste (Ctrl+C/X/V, Ctrl+Insert, Shift+Delete/Insert) through a Clipboard interface (system
  clipboard via AWT, in-memory when headless), word-wrapped Gui.paragraph and TextWrap
- Demo: procedural sky reflected by the PBR spheres (-Ddemo.sky=file.hdr or none), sky light slider

Version 0.6.0
The remaining known limitations addressed. Contains:
- Physics: capsule and triangle-mesh colliders (BVH, per-triangle contacts), ball/hinge (with motor)/distance joints
  that can break, continuous collision for fast bodies, syncQueries, block-solved contact manifolds with central
  friction (tall stacks no longer creep, awake steps a third faster)
- Rendering: HDR target with bloom, ACES tone mapping and FXAA, linear (sRGB-correct) lighting, physically based
  materials (GGX, metallic/roughness maps), spot lights with shadows, cached point/spot shadow maps, weighted blended
  order-independent transparency
- MTL: Ka, Tr, Ni, the PBR extension (Pr, Pm, map_Pr, map_Pm), texture options -s, -o and -clamp
- UI: clipping, scroll regions, Tab focus navigation, text editing with cursor and selection, multi-line text areas,
  glyphs rasterized at the drawn size (sharp scaled text)
- Demo: PBR spheres, spot lamp, crossing glass panes, swinging chain, motorised paddle, mesh-collider dome, fast throws
  with continuous collision, notes area and collision log in the settings panel

Version 0.5.0
Known limitations addressed. Contains:
- Physics: warm-started contact impulses and speculative contacts (12-crate stacks stand without sleeping, 20 with),
  friction solved before the normal rows, compound bodies (mass split by volume, parallel-axis inertia), kinematic
  bodies and static colliders moved by code carry and drag what rests on them, grid-accelerated raycasts and
  overlapSphere (3D DDA)
- Rendering: point light cube shadows, RenderSettings (shadow sizes, point shadow count, occlusion culling), casters far
  towards the sun are kept, mesh tangents with handedness, specular/emissive/height (parallax occlusion) maps, alpha
  cut-outs with matching shadows, moved static geometry detected automatically, box culling for long or flat objects,
  occlusion culling of static grid cells, instanced transparent draws
- MTL: Ke, illum, map_Ks, map_Ke, map_d, disp/map_disp; grey bump maps become normal maps
- UI: proportional fonts and any Unicode glyph (on-demand atlas), several fonts per frame, mouse/wheel/text input,
  immediate-mode widgets (engine.ui.Gui: panels, buttons, checkboxes, sliders, text fields, rows)
- Engine: screenshots (-Dengine.screenshot), CI smoke run of the demo on Linux (Xvfb + Mesa), fewer per-frame allocations
- Demo: settings panel (Esc), turntable, compound dumbbell, cut-out grate, shadow-casting point light, parallax bricks

Version 0.4.0
Visual quality. Contains:
- Tangent-space normal maps (Material.normalMap, MTL map_Bump/bump/norm with -bm, NormalMaps.fromHeights)
- Cascaded shadow maps (1-4 cascades, stable bounding-sphere fitting with texel snapping, cascade blending, fade-out,

Version 0.3.0
Scaling work. Contains:

Version 0.2.0

- Render interpolation between fixed updates

- Resource manager (textures from streams, OBJ loader), AABB collisions, debug title-bar HUD
