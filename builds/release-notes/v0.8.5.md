# v0.8.5: Particles

Fire, smoke, sparks and dust for games built on the engine.

## New
- **Particle systems.** Add a `ParticleEmitter` to an entity and set its fields. It spawns camera-facing quads at a `rate`, or all at once with `burst(n)`, and the renderer draws each emitter in one instanced call.
  - **Shape and motion:** particles start inside a sphere (`shapeRadius`) and fly off in a cone around `direction` (`spreadDegrees`). Set their speed, lifetime, `acceleration` (gravity, or upward for rising flames) and `drag`.
  - **Over their life:** size and colour blend from start to end. `fadeIn` stops new particles from popping up. They can spin. A `brightness` above 1 makes them glow through bloom.
  - **Textures:** with no `texture`, each particle is a soft round dot. A texture can be an atlas of frames, played over each particle's life or picked at random per particle.
  - **Blending:** `ADDITIVE` for fire and sparks, or `ALPHA` for smoke and dust. Alpha particles are sorted back to front, and emitters are sorted against each other.
  - **Soft particles:** particles fade out where they meet walls and the ground (`softness`) instead of cutting in with a hard line.
  - **Simulation:** particles move at the fixed step (they stop while the game is paused) and are interpolated between steps like everything else. While an emitter moves, its particles spread along its path, so trails stay smooth. They stay in the world where they were born, or move with the entity (`localSpace`).
  - Emitters are saved with the scene. The debug text (F3) counts particles and emitters.
- **Demo:** a campfire with flames, embers and drifting smoke. `E` also throws sparks off whatever it pushes.

```java
var fire = new Entity("fire");
var flames = fire.add(new ParticleEmitter());
flames.blend = ParticleEmitter.Blend.ADDITIVE;
flames.rate = 90f;
flames.acceleration.set(0f, 2.5f, 0f);
flames.sizeStart = 0.5f;
flames.sizeEnd = 0.1f;
flames.colorStart.set(1f, 0.75f, 0.3f, 0.8f);
flames.colorEnd.set(0.9f, 0.15f, 0.02f, 0f);
flames.brightness = 2.5f;
scene.add(fire);
```

## Limits
- Particles are unlit: they get no light or shadows and cast no shadows. They do not collide with anything.
- They are drawn after the other transparent surfaces, so a particle behind a glass pane is not dimmed by the glass.

## Use it

```xml
<dependency>
  <groupId>io.github.ad0bert</groupId>
  <artifactId>engine-named-scratch</artifactId>
  <version>0.8.5</version>
</dependency>
```

Everything else is the same as v0.8.4. A scene without emitters renders exactly as before.
