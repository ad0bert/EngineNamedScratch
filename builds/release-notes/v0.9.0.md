# v0.9.0: Characters, navigation and a richer world

Walking characters and agents that find their way, faster iteration, more saved state, remappable controls, better
lighting, terrain and sound effects.

## New
- **Character controller.** Add a `CharacterController` next to an upright `CapsuleCollider`, then set where it walks with `walk(velocity)` and call `jump(speed)`. The physics step moves it by sweeping the capsule.
  - It slides along walls and into corners, steps up ledges up to `stepHeight`, and walks slopes up to `maxSlopeDegrees`. Steeper slopes are walls.
  - It stays on the ground going down slopes and steps (`snapDistance`). A jump still works shortly after walking off a ledge (`coyoteTime`).
  - It rides moving and turning platforms, pushes crates as if it weighed `mass` kilograms, and is pushed out of things that move into it.
  - With a `CharacterController` on the entity, `FirstPersonController` walks instead of flying and pitches a `head` child. `Space` or the gamepad's `Y` jumps.
- **Shape casts and collision layers.**
  - `PhysicsSystem.sphereCast`, `capsuleCast` and `overlapCapsule`, exact at box corners and on mesh triangles.
  - `Collider.layer` (0 to 31) with `setLayersCollide(a, b, false)`. Queries take a layer mask: `PhysicsSystem.solid(PhysicsSystem.layers(1, 2))`.
- **Navigation.** `NavGrid.bake(physics, scene, settings, box)` works out where an agent of a given radius, height, step and slope can walk. It keeps up to four floors per cell, so bridges and storeys work. `findPath` returns straightened paths.
  - Regions can be baked again (`rebake`) and cells blocked at run time (`setBlocked`), for doors and the like.
  - A `NavAgent` walks its entity to `setDestination(point)`, through its character controller if it has one.
- **Hot reloading.** `engine.setHotReload(true)` (or `-Dengine.hotReload=true`) watches the asset directories while the game runs.
  - Changed shaders are compiled again; one that does not compile keeps running its old version. Textures and OBJ meshes reload in place.
  - `engine.onAssetChanged(path -> ...)` hears of every changed file, for reloading a level or a prefab.
  - Shader files in an asset directory (`shaders/tonemap.frag`) replace the built-in ones.
- **Profiler.** `engine.profiler()` times every system, the game's update and rendering per frame. Add your own sections with `begin`/`end`. F3 now shows a frame-time graph and the slowest sections.
- **Scene files keep more.** Animators save their clips and the layers playing (time, speed, weight, fade). Skinned meshes save their skins. Sound sources save whether they play and where. A saved glTF character carries on animating where it was.
- **Controls players can change.**
  - Bindings can be listed, changed and written as text (`W, UP, pad:Y`). `saveBindings`/`loadBindings` keep them in `Settings`.
  - A `Rebinder` binds the next key or gamepad button pressed, for a controls menu.
- **Rumble.** `gamepad.rumble(strong, weak, seconds)`, or `input.rumble(...)` for every pad, on pads whose motors JInput can drive.
- **Rendering.**
  - **Ambient occlusion:** creases, corners and the ground under objects get less sky light. Direct light is unchanged. On by default; `RenderSettings.ambientOcclusion`, `aoRadius` and `aoStrength`.
  - **Reflection probes:** a `ReflectionProbe` captures the room around it. Inside its box, surfaces reflect the room (box-projected) and take their ambient light from it instead of the open sky.
  - **Levels of detail:** `MeshRenderer.addLod(distance, mesh)` and `cullDistance`. `MeshSimplifier` makes the coarser meshes. `RenderSettings.lodBias` scales the distances.
  - **Terrain:** a `Heightfield` (from a function, an image or the built-in noise) becomes chunked `Terrain` with levels of detail and colliders. Characters, physics and navigation walk on it.
  - **Particles:** smoke and dust can be lit (`lit`). Particles can collide with the world (`collider = physics.particleCollider(scene, mask)`) and bounce off it or die where they hit.
- **Sound effects.**
  - **Reverb zones:** while the listener is inside a `ReverbZone`, positioned sounds reverberate like its preset (room, hall, cave, underwater...). Its `lowPass` can muffle everything heard inside.
  - **Occlusion:** sounds behind walls get quieter and duller.
  - Both need OpenAL's EFX extension, which the bundled OpenAL Soft has.
- **Demo:**
  - You walk now (`V` flies), and an orange companion follows you.
  - The settings panel has a Controls section and an ambient occlusion switch.
  - Hills rise behind the field.
  - A room by the field has a mirror-like floor and its own acoustics.
  - Sparks bounce, embers go out on the ground, and the smoke is lit.

```java
var player = new Entity("player");
player.add(new CapsuleCollider(0.3f, 0.6f));
var body = player.add(new CharacterController());
scene.add(player);

var grid = NavGrid.bake(engine.physics(), scene, new NavGrid.Settings(), -50, -5, -50, 50, 20, 50);
var guard = new Entity("guard");
guard.add(new CapsuleCollider(0.3f, 0.6f));
guard.add(new CharacterController());
guard.add(new NavAgent(grid)).setDestination(new Vector3f(10, 0, 4));
scene.add(guard);
```

## Changes
- **Ambient occlusion is on by default**, so scenes look a little darker in corners. Turn it off with `-Dengine.ambientOcclusion=false` or `RenderSettings.ambientOcclusion = false`.
- `Space` is bound to both `up` (flying) and the new `jump` action.
- `playOnStart` no longer restarts a sound that a scene file loaded as already playing.
- Scene files may contain `clips` and `skins` sections. Older engines ignore them and load the rest.
- `RenderBackend`, `AudioBackend` and `AudioVoice` have new default methods, so other implementations keep compiling.

## Fixes
- Rays that run exactly along the face of a mesh collider's bounding box no longer miss it. For example, a vertical ray onto a terrain grid line used to pass through the ground.

## Limits
- Navigation grids are 2.5D: no jump or drop links, and agents do not avoid each other. Baking is synchronous (about 1.5 s for the demo's 60 x 60 m in CI).
- Reflection probes need a sky, see only opaque surfaces, and at most four apply per frame.
- There is one reverb at a time (the zone the listener is deepest in), and occlusion is all or nothing per ray.
- Terrain is not saved in scene files; build it again when loading.
- Hot reloading skips models with their materials, glTF files, sounds, and textures made from several files.

## Use it

```xml
<dependency>
  <groupId>io.github.ad0bert</groupId>
  <artifactId>engine-named-scratch</artifactId>
  <version>0.9.0</version>
</dependency>
```

Games written for v0.8.5 compile unchanged. Apart from ambient occlusion, they look and sound as before until they use the new features.
