# v0.8.4: Glass

A new kind of see-through surface for games built on the engine.

## New
- **Glass materials.** `material.glass(alpha)` (or `glass = true` on a transparent material) makes a surface that adds its own light in full, its reflections of the sky, highlights and glow, however clear it is. Only the light from behind it is cut, by its coverage. The coverage is the colour's alpha where you look straight through and rises to opaque edge-on (Fresnel), so a clear pane shows mostly towards its edges, as real glass does.
  - Before, a nearly clear transparent surface faded its reflections along with everything else, so thin glass was either invisible or looked like fog.
  - Looks best with the metallic-roughness model, a low roughness and a dark colour, which tints the glass: `new Material().color(0.02f, 0.05f, 0.05f, 1f).pbr(0f, 0.03f).glass(0.035f)`.
  - Works with both ways of drawing transparency: order-independent and sorted back to front.

## Use it

```xml
<dependency>
  <groupId>io.github.ad0bert</groupId>
  <artifactId>engine-named-scratch</artifactId>
  <version>0.8.4</version>
</dependency>
```

Nothing else changed from v0.8.3. Existing materials look the same.
